# Visual Style

Resolve the **latest effective template**. The current default is wide-v3 at960×540pt; read [layered-template-v3.md](layered-template-v3.md) for precise assets, geometry and layer controls. Use [legacy-visual-style.md](legacy-visual-style.md) only for the selected old720×405pt template.

The **first slide** receives the **highest visual scrutiny** and establishes alignment, palette, typography and scene treatment. Keep important church-name, vision and service text editable; use **editable emphasis shapes** or typographic grouping where they improve hierarchy. Maintain pale upper negative space in the **top half** for **front-row sightlines**, with scenery at right-middle and uncomplicated natural gray-lilac lower quarter. Do not repeat a **dominant dark-purple block** across unrelated pages.

Design by content: water for baptism, bread/wheat for communion, sanctuary for welcome, and a quiet content-related landscape for scripture. Keep one ivory, pearl, pale lavender and limestone family. Objects must clear actual reading and authentic QR/form/photo areas. Do not bake text, identity, footer, subtitle cloud or a solid purple bottom into generated art. For prompt-only redraws preserve original generated files and regenerate compositions that fail instead of painting repair patches.

Current scripture samples use SimSun38pt; match uploaded fonts first and choose explicit fitting sizes for other content. Keep dark aubergine on light backgrounds and restrained close font shadows without glow. Titles, paragraphs and subtitles remain editable. Preserve the exact complete Logo, compact one-line Chinese and one-line English names, low foreground and optional cloud layers. Church identity has its own Noto Serif SC/Garamond fonts.

Give layouts unique Name and MatchingName. No separate logo-tip extraction is needed with the current complete logo. Keep the empty named subtitle field available; delete only unused generic title/subtitle/date/footer placeholders. Test both cloud states and true PowerPoint duplication after save/reopen.
